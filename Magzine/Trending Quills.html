Modern Storytelling On
Social Media:

When oversharing drowns the story


We live in a world where every moment is a story — and every story wants an audience.

With one tap, we post. A selfie becomes a statement. A random thought becomes a reel. A heartbreak becomes a caption. And slowly, silently, our most personal moments turn into content.

This is modern storytelling on social media — fast, filtered, and constantly needing validation.

But here’s the truth we rarely speak about: in the rush to share everything, we’re forgetting what it means to truly feel something.

We perform our lives. We narrate every meal, every mood, every minute like we're characters in a show. Somewhere along the way,the story stopped being about us — and started being about how it looks to others.

A teen writes a deep caption, but deletes it if it doesn’t get enough likes. A girl posts photos of her “perfect” day, while silently battling anxiety. A young artist shares their poem, but can’t enjoy the praise because they’re comparing it to someone else’s viral post.

When every story must be public, what happens to the parts of ourselves that need privacy to grow?

Stories used to be sacred — shared in hushed voices, trusted circles, handwritten diaries. Now they’re hashtags.

And in this blur, we risk losing:

• Authenticity:When we share for claps instead of connection.
• Presence:When we capture the moment but forget to live it.
• Depth:When everything must fit into 30 seconds, the deeper emotions get edited out.

This isn’t to say storytelling online is all bad — in fact, it’s powerful. It has united voices, raised awareness, and sparked creativity. But like all power, it needs balance.

So maybe the next time you feel something big — joy, heartbreak, wonder — you don’t rush to post it.
Maybe you sit with it.
Maybe you write it down for yourself.
Maybe you let it breathe before turning it into a reel.

Because not every story needs to be told out loud to be real.

Some of the best ones are still being written — quietly, off-screen, just for you.
