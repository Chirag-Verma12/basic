Poetry

Nazaare kuch yun bayan kar jaate hain, Dil ki baatein bhi asar kar jaate hain.

Bas ek shakhs ki aankhon mein dekho, Hazaron raaz wo bayan kar jaate hain.

Kabhi in nigaahon mein jhaank ke dekho, Duniya ko kis rang mein piro jaate hain.

Aur kabhi inhi mein doob jaaye koi, Toh mohabbat ke afsane likhe jaate hain..

mujhe chand dekhna pasand hai usse chand jasa dikhna pasand hai wo bhi apne daag nhi chupati aakhon mai kajal mathe pe bindi nhi lagti

hothon ko unhi ki haal pai chor deti hai muskuraate hi samjh ke saare baandh tod deti hai ussy kyu sikhaye sajhna sawarna

ussy kyu sikhaye sajhna sawarna badal na jaaye iss duniya ka haal warna main khush hu

main khush hu ki wo aayeine se apna haal nhi puchti ye-wo haan-nahi befizul sawaal nhi puchti socho aagar wo kajal laga le

socho aagar wo kajal laga le ek dafa bas uljhe baal suljha le ye jamaana apna rukh na badal de har koi uske saath na chal de ye haawaaein ruke he jaaye ussey dekhne ko kahin maine jasbse dekha hai main hoo wahin
