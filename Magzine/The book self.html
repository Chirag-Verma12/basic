Picks

Stories that stayed with us.

As editors, we come across hundreds of books — some pass like passing rain, while others leave footprints on our soul. This issue, we're sharing the titles that did more than entertain. These are the books that made us pause, reflect, and fall in love with storytelling all over again.

Whether you're curled up on a rainy day or looking for something that makes your heart beat a little faster, here are our top picks for the season:

The Midnight Library by Matt Haig:

A beautiful, thought-provoking novel about choices, regrets, and the infinite lives we could live. This book is a gentle reminder that even the small moments have meaning.

On Earth We’re Briefly Gorgeous by Ocean Vuong:

This isn’t just a novel — it’s a poem written with fire. It explores identity, family, trauma, and love in a way that will leave you breathless.

Big Magic by Elizabeth Gilbert:

For every artist, writer, or creative soul who feels blocked or afraid, this book is a warm, wise companion. It doesn’t just speak to your mind — it speaks to your courage.

The Book Thief by Markus Zusak:

Set in Nazi Germany, told through the eyes of Death, this novel is a haunting yet heartwarming ode to the power of books and stories — even in the darkest times.

The Little Prince by Antoine de Saint-Exupéry:

A short classic, but one that holds deep truths about life, love, and what really matters — seen through the eyes of a child, yet speaking directly to the hearts of adults.
