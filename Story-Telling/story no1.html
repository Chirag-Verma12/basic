The Unforgetable Story

The Lost Key

Once upon a time in a quiet village, a curious boy named Aarav found an old, rusty key buried beneath a tree in his backyard. It had strange symbols carved on it, unlike anything he had ever seen. That night, he had a dream about a hidden door in the forest. The next morning, driven by excitement, Aarav followed the path from his dream—and to his amazement, he found the door inside the trunk of a giant oak tree. With a trembling hand, he used the key. The door creaked open, revealing a small room glowing with soft golden light. Inside were shelves of ancient books, jars of glowing stones, and a letter that read: "To the one who dares to seek, may knowledge and kindness guide your path." From that day, Aarav visited the magical room often, learning and growing. But he never took anything out—because he understood that some treasures are meant to be protected, not possessed. And so, the boy who found a key became the keeper of a secret world.
