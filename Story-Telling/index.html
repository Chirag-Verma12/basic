Story Telling

By - Chirag Verma

Title: "The Last Lantern"

In a forgotten village veiled by eternal night, only one lantern remained lit. Every home had surrendered to the creeping shadows—except for old Mara’s. Her cottage glowed dimly atop a hill, where the lantern burned with a gentle, golden flame. People whispered that Mara had made a pact with light itself. Children dared each other to visit her, but none ever returned the same. Not because of fear, but because of something else—hope. One stormy evening, a boy named Elian climbed the hill. “Why does your lantern never die?” he asked. Mara smiled, eyes reflecting the flame. “Because someone must remember the sun.” She handed Elian a tiny spark from the lantern, captured in glass. “Take this to your home. Keep it burning, and tell others.” The boy left, his heart warm. Soon, more homes lit up, their windows flickering like stars. Years later, the village no longer feared the dark. Instead, they learned to carry light within them—passing it from hand to hand, story to story. And Mara’s lantern? It finally dimmed, but by then, it no longer mattered. The village had become a constellation of hope—lighting the world from the inside out.

Motive: Hope in Darkness
